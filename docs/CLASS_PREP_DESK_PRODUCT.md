**Related:** `CLASSROOM_HOME_PRODUCT.md` (yellow Welcome / Wrap), `BOOKS_LIBRARY_PRODUCT.md` (workshop), `READING_CHECKS_PRODUCT.md`, `BOOK_EXERCISES_PRODUCT.md`, `STUDENT_HOME_PRODUCT.md`.

---

## Why

Yellow Welcome is the **kid’s stage**. Book workshop is **once per story**. Prepare was doing both jobs. Split them.

---

## Three doors, one book

| Door | Job | Clock |
|------|-----|--------|
| **Workshop** (Books / Stories) | Scan, checks, vocab, boxing, outline. Reused for every student. | Off. No yellow. |
| **Today’s class** (Prepare) | This kid, this hour. Teacher sheet. | Off until Start (or auto-start). |
| **Student stage** | Yellow Welcome / Wrap, then the live reader. | On when live. |

Same PDF reader. Continue from Prepare = this kid’s last page, clock off. Live Continue = last page, clock on. Workshop Open book = factory pages.

Never show **“Prep”** as a student-facing title. Welcome / Welcome back only on yellow.

---

## What the desk contains

Teacher sheet. Light Apple-like chrome (not yellow). Hide empty rows.

| Block | v1 | Later |
|-------|----|--------|
| Who / when | Name, time, length | — |
| Today’s place | Book · unit · lesson · last page | — |
| This lesson | Parts in outline order; one **start here**; tap a part to see saved words / story / grammar | Ready/not chips |
| This class tweaks | Skip a part, star words for this hour, notes | Last time / review words |
| Actions | **Continue** (clock off) · **Preview** · Done | Ready/not + jump |

You pick **one start piece** (already stored on the class). The rest of the lesson is listed — not a tick-every-part planner. Not a reading/writing/listening form.

**See the fuel, don’t rebuild it:** the desk shows what is already saved on the book. Empty if nothing is saved. **Open in Books** jumps to Stories / part prep. Do not scan, generate, or rewrite packs on Prepare.

**This-class vs book:** start here, skip, starred words, and notes belong to this kid this hour. Word meanings and story text stay on the book.

**Status vs jump vs in-book:** later, desk shows ready/not; tap jumps to workshop; boxing and running checks happen in the book.

---

## Yellow home

Welcome / Wrap stay student-facing. **Preview** from the desk is the same Welcome, read-only. **Enter** (when class is about to start) / auto-start → live Welcome. Prepare does **not** start the clock.

Do not scan / generate / approve on yellow. Do not edit today’s lines on yellow (move in a later phase).

- Skill-category planner (reading / writing / listening as the structure)
