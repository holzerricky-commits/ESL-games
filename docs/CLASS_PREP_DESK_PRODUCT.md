# Prepare — product decisions (locked)

Last updated: 2026-09-16

**Status:** Locked. The separate **Today’s class** teacher-sheet lobby is **removed**. Do not rebuild it.

**UI names:** **Prepare** (the door) · the book spread (the work surface)

**Related:** `CLASSROOM_HOME_PRODUCT.md` (yellow Welcome / Wrap), `BOOKS_LIBRARY_PRODUCT.md` (workshop), `READING_CHECKS_PRODUCT.md`, `BOOK_EXERCISES_PRODUCT.md`, `STUDENT_HOME_PRODUCT.md`.

---

## Why

Yellow Welcome is the **kid’s stage**. Book workshop is **once per story**. Prepare is **this kid, this hour**, on the same PDF reader, clock off.

A lobby sheet between the roster and the book (parts list, briefing, skip/star, Preview) was an extra stop. Skip/star and a word briefing were waiting on a **vocabulary bank** that is not built yet. Until that exists, prep is the book.

---

## Three doors, one book

| Door | Job | Clock |
|------|-----|--------|
| **Workshop** (Books / Stories) | Scan, checks, vocab, boxing, outline. Reused for every student. | Off. No yellow. |
| **Prepare** | This kid, this hour. Opens the teaching book at last page. | Off until Enter / auto-start. |
| **Student stage** | Yellow Welcome / Wrap, then the live reader. | On when live. |

Never show **“Prep”** as a student-facing title. Welcome / Welcome back only on yellow.

---

## What Prepare does now

- Lands on the **book spread** (last page), clock off. Capsule: Checks · Save · Leave.
- Save / Leave / close / Escape return to the **student Classes tab** — not yellow Welcome, not a teacher sheet.
- Notes and “what we’re teaching” stay on the **student Classes tab**.
- Do not scan, generate, or rewrite packs on Prepare. That stays in Books.

---

## What NOT to rebuild yet

Do **not** add a Prepare landing page, Preview Welcome, skip-this-part, star-words briefing, or part-content sheet until the student **vocabulary bank** (and whatever this-hour words actually mean) exists. Then design on the spread, not as a lobby.

Do not:

- AI lesson planner (roadmap Phase 5)
- Lesson Hub / second book viewer
- Rebuild Books on Prepare
- Use yellow Welcome as the prep landing
