# Lesson vault — product

Last updated: 2026-10-07

**Phased plan:** `LESSON_VAULT_PHASED_PLAN.md`

## What it is

While reading with a student, a new word comes up. You explain it, click it, and it goes into a **vault** for that lesson. At the end of class you open the vault and review the cards together.

## Locked decisions

- **One vault per student + lesson.** The part (main story, paired story, vocabulary page) is a small label on each card, not a separate vault.
- **Save is instant.** No form before the word goes in. Picture and a better sentence can be added later, on the card.
- **Sentence comes from the page.** The line around the word becomes the back of the card. If the page text is messy, the sentence stays empty and you fix it later.
- **Picture is optional** and comes from the existing picture search (Phase 3).
- **Separate from:** the prepared interactive vocabulary on a book part (shared, set before class), the translate-dock saved words, and the short “needs practice / going well” word-review list.

## Navigation

- **Saving** files the word into the lesson of the page the word is on.
- **Closed vault control** shows the count for the lesson of the page you are on. It changes at real lesson boundaries only (vocabulary pages → story in the same lesson does not change it).
- **Open vault** stays on the lesson you are viewing. Page turns do not swap the cards. If the page is now another lesson, the panel says so and offers **Show this page’s lesson**.
- Inside the vault, **previous / next lesson** switches the vault without turning the book.
- Saving a word for a different lesson than the one open switches the vault to that lesson so the new card is visible.

## Cards

- Front: picture (Phase 3) + word.
- Back (Phase 2 flip): one example sentence, optional short meaning.

## Non-goals (v1)

- Per-story vaults or folders inside folders.
- Feeding vault words into the long-term vocabulary bank (later pass).
