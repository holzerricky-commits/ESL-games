# Book exercises — product decisions (locked)

**UI names (teacher-facing):** Exercises · Box a task · Word bank · Choose answer · Check

**Milestone tie-in:** Interactive book sibling to reading checks — for **exercise-driven** books (exam / workbook), not stories.

**Related:** `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` (book-first, 1:1 screen share), `MILESTONE.md`, reading checks (speak→mark, different job).

---

## What this is

On a **workbook page**, you mark **one numbered exercise** so class can later do it as a focused activity with **Check answers** (green/red).

You pick the type **before** you box: **Word bank** or **Choose the correct answer**. Gemini never picks the type.

The book stays on screen. No new browser tab.

---

## How class runs (v1)

You share the screen and click. The student tells you what to tap. Word bank: what goes in each blank. Choose answer: which choice.

---

## Three pieces (keep separate)

| Piece | Job |
|------|-----|
| **Task box** | Which blob on which PDF page is this exercise. |
| **Activity** | Word bank: bank + gapped sentences. Choose answer: questions + choices + one right answer. |
| **Live Check** | Focused sheet in class; Check; close; still on the book. |

AI only **drafts** the activity from a crop of the box. You always approve. Draft never goes live.

---

## Where it lives

| Surface | Role |
|---------|------|
| **Open book** (Today’s class Continue, workshop, or live class) | Box a task on the page; list of tasks; pins; Draft from box; Check sheet. |

Today’s class desk only shows ready/not later — boxing stays on the page.

Do **not** merge into reading checks or timed challenges.

---

## Locked

- You pick **Word bank** or **Choose answer** before boxing. Gemini does not pick the type.
- You frame **one** exercise (not a whole unit scan).
- **Word-bank v1 stays.** Tap or drag a word onto an empty gap (snap when close). Drag a placed word back to the bank or onto another empty gap. Draft from box and Check stay word-bank until a later choose-answer phase.
- **Choose the correct answer:** one or more questions in that box. Each question has a prompt, **2–4 choices**, and **exactly one** right answer. In class you tap a choice, then Check → green/red. Same pin, draft, Approve, and Check loop.
- **Not reading checks.** Story checks are “student speaks, you mark.” This is a workbook task with a keyed answer you Check on the book. Separate list, pins, and save files.
- Open-ended / type-in / multi-select are later. Open-ended cannot auto-Check.
- Teacher navigation is never blocked.

---

## Success (word-bank Phase 5)

You Check in class, see green/red, can Show answers or Try again, and extra unused words plus two-gap sentences work. Draft still never plays until you approve.

Choose-answer success is the same loop once that type’s phases ship: Check, green/red, Show answers, draft never live until you approve.
